What this policy covers

This policy describes the public GlueTec company website hosted at jiho00.github.io/GlueTec. It does not cover a future LAB OS application, research pilot, or paid service; those will need information specific to their actual data handling before onboarding.

This website has no account system, contact form, newsletter signup, checkout, advertising trackers, or visitor analytics added by GlueTec. The LAB OS demonstration uses illustrative content and does not collect or upload research records.

Website hosting and access records

GitHub Pages delivers this website. Your browser connects to GitHub’s infrastructure to request its pages and assets. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub.

GitHub handles hosting-related information under its own privacy practices, which may include technical request and device information. Its storage locations, retention, and rights-request procedures are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. See also GitHub Pages data collection.

GlueTec has not added a separate visitor tracking system. We do not control GitHub’s hosting logs or set their retention period.

Cookies and your motion preference

GlueTec’s website code does not set cookies. If you choose “Motion on” or “Motion off,” it saves that choice in your browser’s local storage under gluetec-motion, with the value on or off.

This preference stays in that browser until you change it or clear the site’s storage. Our code does not send it to GlueTec or use it to identify or track you. Without a saved choice, the homepage follows your device’s reduced-motion preference.

You can change the setting using the homepage’s motion control or remove it through your browser’s site-data settings. If storage is blocked, the site still works. Fonts and artwork are served with the site; we do not load Google Fonts or an advertising service in your browser.

Information you choose to share

If you contact the team through GitHub or a subsequently agreed private channel, the information you choose to provide may be used to answer your request and follow up on the conversation. GitHub’s own privacy settings and policies apply to interactions on GitHub.

Please share only what is needed for the inquiry. We aim to keep correspondence only as needed to handle the request, maintain an ongoing relationship, or meet applicable obligations; storage in an external service also follows that service’s retention rules.

Links to other services

Following links to GitHub or scientific publications takes you to services with their own data practices. Review their privacy information before providing personal information. This policy describes GlueTec’s website, rather than those external services.

Your choices and requests

You can browse without creating an account, choose reduced motion, and clear the locally stored preference. For information you have shared directly with the team, you may ask about access, correction, or deletion through the contact route below. Applicable rights and any retention obligations depend on the request and relevant law.

Requests about GitHub’s own hosting records should follow the contact and rights procedures in its Privacy Statement.

Contact and policy changes

Use the GlueTec contact section to reach our current public contact route, Jiho Lee’s GitHub profile. A dedicated company email is not currently listed.

GitHub issues and public discussions are visible to others. Do not post personal information, confidential research, or patient data there. For a request involving private information, first ask for an appropriate private contact method without including that information.

If the site’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. New forms, analytics, or LAB OS access should be accompanied by privacy information that reflects those features.
